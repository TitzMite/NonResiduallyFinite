
The BMW-group GammaCV is taken from "Fractal Anti-Tori" by Caprace
and Vast: Corollary 1.3.

It is irreducible and the local actions are nilpotent. In GammaCV is not
resiudally finite (Indicability, Residual Finiteness, and Simple
Subquotients of Groups Acting on Trees, Caprace--Weselok).

We used the search method to find a complex whose fundamental group
is a C2-tilde lattice and contains the type-preserving subgroup of GammaCV.
In particular the C2-tilde lattice is virtually simple (Lecureux--Witzel,
The Normal Subgroup Theorem for lattices on two-dimensional Euclidean buildings).
